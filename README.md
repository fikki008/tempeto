# Tempeto 🗓️

**Plan your day. Make it count.**

Tempeto is a simple daily planner. Add your tasks, set times if you want, tick them off, and watch your progress grow through the day.

The name comes from French *temps* (time) and Yoruba *ètò* (plan).

---

## What you can do

- **Add, edit and delete tasks.** Times are optional, so a task can be scheduled or unscheduled.
- **See duration automatically.** For example, 8:00 AM to 9:30 AM shows as 1 hr 30 min.
- **Mark tasks as important** with the star ★.
- **Get an overlap warning** if two tasks clash, and choose to save anyway or go back.
- **Track your progress.** The bar changes colour as you finish more tasks.
- **Tap the summary cards** (Total, Completed, Pending) to filter your list.
- **Switch dates** with the date picker. Every day has its own tasks and progress.
- **Celebrate!** Finish every task in a day and a message appears.
- **Completed History.** Clear finished tasks from your day and they are kept in History. You can remove any single task from History, with an Undo option.

---

## Where your data is saved

Everything is saved in your browser (Local Storage), so your tasks are still there after you refresh or close the page.

Your tasks belong to the browser and device you used. They won't appear on another phone or computer, and clearing your browser data will erase them.
